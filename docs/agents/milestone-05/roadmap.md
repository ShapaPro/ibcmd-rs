The completed [#408](https://github.com/Untru/ibcmd-rs/issues/408) and
[#416](https://github.com/Untru/ibcmd-rs/issues/416) fixes are present in the
v0.4.0 baseline. So are the reviewed F-1/F-2/F-3/F-9/F-10/F-15 fixes,
extension export parity and whole-tree extension loading. The saved dynamic
feature and live research kit had not been integrated; this wave recovers
those changes without resetting their original worktrees.

`Params .ui` records are licensing records, not a generation-selection codec
to implement. See [the measured investigation](../../apply/params-ui.md).
That result removes an old hypothesis; it does not by itself prove online or
live activation on 8.5.
